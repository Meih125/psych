Psych

Privacy policy

Psych is a personal habit tracker. It has no account, no server and no ads.

Health data

With your permission, Psych reads your daily step count from Health Connect. It uses the numbers only to show your progress and to give in-app rewards (XP and gold).

Psych does not read any other health data, does not write to Health Connect, and never sends your data anywhere. Everything stays on this phone, except backup files that you choose to save yourself.

Turning it off

You can remove Psych's access at any time in Health Connect › App permissions › Psych, or tap Disconnect in Psych's settings.
